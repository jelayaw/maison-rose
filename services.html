Our Services

Thoughtful Cleaning.
Beautiful Results.

From routine care to detailed resets, our services are designed to give your home the attention it needs and you a little more room to breathe.

Made for Real Life

Find the Right Care
for Your Home.

Every home is different. Whether you need dependable recurring care, a detailed deep clean, help during a move, or a reliable turnover for your short-term rental, Maison Rose offers thoughtful service tailored to your space.

01

Routine Care

Standard Cleaning

Designed for homes that need dependable upkeep on a recurring or occasional basis. Standard cleaning focuses on the everyday areas that help your home feel fresh, comfortable, and maintained.

What's Included

  • Dusting accessible surfaces
  • Kitchen counters and exterior surfaces
  • Bathroom cleaning and sanitizing
  • Vacuuming carpets and rugs
  • Sweeping and mopping floors
  • General tidying and finishing touches
02

Extra Attention

Deep Cleaning

Perfect for homes that need more than routine maintenance. Our deep cleaning service gives extra attention to frequently overlooked areas and detailed surfaces.

What's Included

  • Detailed kitchen and bathroom cleaning
  • Appliance exteriors and cabinet fronts
  • Baseboards, doors, and trim
  • Ceiling fans and accessible fixtures
  • High-touch surface sanitizing
  • Detailed floors and finishing touches
03

A Fresh Beginning

Move-In / Move-Out

Whether you're settling into a new home or preparing one for its next occupants, this service provides a thorough reset designed for empty or transitioning spaces.

What's Included

  • Cabinet and drawer interiors when empty
  • Baseboards, doors, and trim
  • Kitchen and bathroom detailing
  • Interior surfaces and fixtures
  • Thorough floor cleaning
  • Final room-by-room finishing check
04

Guest Ready

Airbnb Turnovers

Reliable turnover cleaning designed for short-term rental owners who need their property refreshed, reset, and ready to welcome the next guest.

What's Included

  • Cleaning and resetting guest spaces
  • Bathroom and kitchen sanitizing
  • Changing provided linens and towels
  • Vacuuming, sweeping, and mopping
  • Checking for visible guest mess or concerns
  • Final guest-ready presentation
