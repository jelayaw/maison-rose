Meet Keisha

The Heart Behind
Maison Rose.

A love for beautiful spaces.

Maison Rose began with Keisha's love for creating spaces that feel calm, welcoming, and cared for. What started as helping friends and families maintain their homes grew into a passion for providing thoughtful, dependable cleaning services throughout the Savannah community.

Her approach is simple: pay attention to the details, respect every home, and make sure each client feels comfortable with the person they welcome into their space.
